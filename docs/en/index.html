How to tie

  1. 1

    Place it on your waist

    The wide part at the front, the two straps behind.

  2. 2

    Wrap the straps

    Cross them around your waist and bring them back to the front over the belt.

  3. 3

    Tie it your way

    A bow at the centre or to the side, or a simple knot with the ends falling.

Questions you ask a lot 💬

How do I place an order on the site?

1. Tap “Add to bag” under the belt you like 👜 2. Tap the bag icon at the top 🛍️ 3. Write your name, number and address, then tap “Send order on WhatsApp” ✅ WhatsApp opens with your order ready — just send it and we’ll call you to confirm. Or simply send us a photo of the belt on WhatsApp and we’ll take it from there 💬

How do I pay?

Either cash on delivery to the courier, or an InstaPay transfer to 01221988192 — then send the screenshot on WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take, and how much?

We deliver to every governorate in Egypt within 3 working days. Standard shipping is 80 EGP, express is 120 EGP, and standard shipping is free on orders from 1500 EGP.

Will it fit me?

The belt is 14 cm wide and 140 cm long, and it wraps and ties, so it fits comfortably up to 90 kg. Need a custom size? Write it in the notes and we will make it.

What is it made of?

Top-grade imported PU leather — soft and light, and the plain belts have soft stretch that shapes to your waist. There are also lace, croc and snake designs.

Can I get a belt made to my size in natural leather?

Yes. Alongside our PU belts, we make belts to measure in natural leather: message us that you're interested, and if you're in Cairo or Giza we'll send one of our team to take your measurements, show you the leathers in person and agree the details and price with you. Then we make it to your exact size and deliver it to you. The service is available in Cairo and Giza only for now.

What if I don’t like it?

You have 14 days from delivery to return it for a full refund or exchange it. If it is faulty or we sent the wrong item, all shipping is on us.
